# Le Petit Chat

Tagline: “A small adventure among the stars.”

The French title and English tagline are confirmed. All menus, dialogue and gameplay text remain in English. The concept below is approved as the basis for the first prototype.

## Confirmed direction

A mysterious, funny space exploration game illustrated in 3D. The player is an astronaut who discovers a finite cast of hidden cats, learns their personalities, meets their needs and earns skills along the way. Completing the adventure means finding every cat and meeting each cat's individual needs.

All player-facing text, dialogue, actions and instructions must be in English.

The portfolio will show a small preview in Playground with a link to a dedicated game page.

## Astronaut

The user leaves gender and appearance open, with an astronaut suit as the only visual requirement.

Proposed design: a woman in an ivory space suit with blue accents and a round reflective visor. No character customization required for the first version. Use “you” in dialogue; a personal name is optional.

Proposed motivation: follow an unexplained signal that appears to be a distress call, but turns out to involve several very demanding cats.

## Visual direction and tone

Confirmed: 2D illustration, mystery and humour.

Proposed: top-down exploration on a small abandoned observatory moon, with deep blue shadows, cyan lights, strange plants and warm shelters. Environmental clues create mystery; feline behaviour and short astronaut observations supply humour.

Confirmed title: “Le Petit Chat”, echoing the tender, mysterious tone of Le Petit Prince.

## The first three cats

All three enjoy food. Feeding alone does not complete a friendship. Respecting a cat's boundaries must be a valid way to succeed, without requiring every cat to accept cuddles.

### 1. Mad Nebula (proposed name)

Confirmed appearance: mostly black with a little white.

Confirmed personality: stubborn, independent, dislikes cuddles and scratches when bothered.

Proposed encounter: sitting on the observatory's control console, preventing an extremely important button from being pressed.

Proposed interaction: offer food, let him decide when to approach, offer a toy when receptive and step back when his tail lashes. Cuddles are never required. A rejected cuddle triggers a brief swipe animation and a humorous reaction, without damage or loss of progress.

Example English line: “The captain has rejected your application for cuddles.”

Proposed completion: fed, comfortable and voluntarily staying near the astronaut, with his personal space respected.

### 2. Scarry Comet (proposed name)

Keep the spelling “Scarry” requested by the user.

Confirmed appearance: orange, slender and agile.

Confirmed personality: fearful, watches from a distance and approaches very reluctantly.

Proposed encounter: glimpses of an orange tail between moon rocks, always just beyond reach.

Proposed interaction: place food nearby, back away and wait. Repeated calm encounters reduce the distance at which he feels safe. Sudden approaches make him retreat, without erasing earned trust. Offer play only after he becomes comfortable.

Example English line: “You have been observed. From a very safe distance.”

Proposed completion: fed and confident enough to approach voluntarily and play. Cuddles are optional, never a requirement.

### 3. Memmette Moon (proposed name)

Confirmed appearance: white with a very small amount of black.

Confirmed personality: older, loyal and wise; loves cuddles and naps.

Proposed encounter: sleeping in the observatory's warm greenhouse beneath a suspiciously cat-shaped star map.

Proposed interaction: offer food when awake, gentle cuddles and a quiet resting place. Let her sleep rather than waking her repeatedly. Once comfortable, she accompanies the astronaut briefly and offers a clue through her behaviour.

Example English line: “Ancient wisdom. Currently unavailable due to a nap.”

Proposed completion: fed, cuddled while receptive and peacefully resting in a safe spot.

## Proposed first playable scope

These implementation choices remain proposals, not completed features:

- One compact explorable zone containing all three cats.
- Top-down movement with keyboard controls and a simple interaction menu; touch controls to be planned.
- Actions: “Offer food”, “Cuddle”, “Play” and “Give space”. Availability depends on the encounter.
- Visible behavioural cues, brief English feedback and a journal of discovered cats.
- Local browser save; no account required.
- Needs advance during play, without penalties while the game is closed.
- No timed pressure or combat.

## Progression proposals to refine together

Earn skills through understanding cats rather than repeating actions. Possible abilities: reading moods more clearly, spotting hidden tracks and discovering new paths. Decide the exact unlock sequence before implementing exploration gates.
